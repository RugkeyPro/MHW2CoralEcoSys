# Data selection, attribution and reuse

The nine source files are unchanged **derived research outputs** selected from the accepted coral project. Six CSV tables and three GeoTIFFs are bundled so that the numerical example and browser demo need no private project paths or remote scientific data service.

The source selection is deliberately small: future habitat quality and MPEI member/ensemble summaries, the repaired September 6 conservation table, historical MHW summary data and three actual future-quality rasters. It is not the full research archive. The SHA-256 and original project-relative locations are recorded in `provenance.json`.

The source tables contain only numerical regional/model summaries, not personal identifiers. The basemap comes from public-domain Natural Earth cartography through the `world-atlas` package. It is separate from the actual project-derived model rasters.

The MIT software license does not grant rights to any underlying third-party source dataset. Full source archives, external occurrence/range records, environmental NetCDFs, reef/MPA raw boundaries and manuscripts are not redistributed by this demo. Consult their original owners and conditions for reuse. For scientific reuse of these derived results, cite this repository, the exact commit and the corresponding source file/version, and contact the research team for the underlying study and complete methods.

Copies under `public/data/source/` and `demo/data/source/` are included solely to make browser downloads and standalone viewing work; their bytes are checked against this same provenance manifest.
